CD Music Display

Let's connect your Spotify account. First, you'll need to create a Spotify Developer App.

1
Create a Spotify App

Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard and create a new app.

2
Set Redirect URI

In your app settings, add this redirect URI:
http://localhost:3000/auth/callback

3
Enter your credentials below

Copy the Client ID and Client Secret from your Spotify app.

Change this if running on a different host/port (e.g. http://192.168.1.50:3000)

CD Music Display

Your Spotify credentials are configured. Connect your account to begin.
